01 / PLATFORM OVERVIEW

From field signal to
trusted intelligence.

Field teams need useful information quickly, but speed cannot come at the cost of security or data quality. Aegis brings the workflow together: authorize, locate, validate, and distribute.

5,000+Designed target / simulated scale
10K+Reported workload target

99.95%Reported service objective
⌖

Know where data belongs

Geospatial indexing helps teams find detections within the areas they are authorized to access, without sending large datasets unnecessarily.

◈

Prove who can see it

JWT/OAuth2 authentication and role-based, per-unit access scopes protect sensitive detections and keep users within their assigned boundaries.

✓

Validate before sharing

Confidence checks route questionable detections to human review. Validated findings can then be published to downstream consumers.
